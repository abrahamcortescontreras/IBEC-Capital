Our origin

IBEC Capital was born from a university project by four friends who shared a passion for markets, portfolio theory, and financial education. After co-founding Zona Financiera — a financial literacy initiative that reached over 17,000 people across Mexico — the team took the next step: putting capital to work under a structured, disciplined framework.

Our mission

To design and manage bespoke portfolios for diverse risk profiles, optimising risk-adjusted returns through rigorous top-down macroeconomic analysis, fundamental research, and disciplined asset allocation.
